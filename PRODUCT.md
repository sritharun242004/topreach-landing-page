# Top Reach Pro

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js and Neon Postgres. The user explicitly requested Next.js. Hosting is undecided.

## Users

Students in Tamil Nadu who want help choosing and trying a practical digital skill. Top Reach Pro teammates review registrations in a private admin area.

## Product Purpose

Help students discover a suitable skill, experience practical work, develop it through guided tasks, and understand possible career or income steps. The landing page explains the program and collects registrations.

## Operating Context

Visitors watch the program video, review the offer and six program elements, hear a community voice, and register with name, phone, email, and city. Teammates review and export those records.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The registration form stores four student details and consent time in Neon.
- A separate, unlinked admin URL with an email and password protects the registration list and CSV export.
- A thank-you page confirms submission and opens the supplied WhatsApp group invite after eight seconds. The visitor can join immediately, stay on the page, or copy a personalized greeting to paste in the group.
- The latest owner-supplied screenshot and PDF specify a 45-day first-income goal. Present it as a goal shaped by the student's work and opportunities, not a guaranteed outcome. An older HTML reference says five days and is superseded for this claim.
- The supplied horizontal video is provisionally the program explanation and the vertical video the community voice. Their roles need owner confirmation.
- The supplied WhatsApp group invite is linked after registration. No public team contact address has been provided.
- User-supplied accomplishments for the landing page: 5.5M+ views achieved, 1,000+ students trained, 1,000+ pieces of content published, and three consecutive batches.

## Brand Commitments

The supplied eagle logo and name Top Reach Pro are preserved. The requested palette is black, white or cream, and sunset orange. The requested visual reference is a Claude-like warm, clear design language, adapted for this brand.

## Product Principles

1. Show students a practical path before asking them to register.
2. Make the call to action easy to understand and use on a phone.
3. Treat income as a goal shaped by effort and opportunity.
4. Keep student data accessible only to the team.
